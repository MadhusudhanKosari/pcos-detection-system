Understanding PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome: Symptoms, Diagnosis & Management

What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting 1 in 10 women of reproductive age. It involves hormonal imbalance, metabolism problems, and ovarian dysfunction.

Key Characteristics:

  • Multiple small cysts on ovaries
  • Hormonal imbalance (high androgens)
  • Irregular menstrual cycles
  • Weight management challenges
Common Symptoms

Menstrual Irregularities

Infrequent, prolonged, or absent periods

Weight Issues

Unexplained weight gain, difficulty losing weight

Skin & Hair

Acne, excessive hair growth, hair thinning

Diagnosis Criteria (Rotterdam Criteria)

Diagnosis requires at least 2 of these 3 features:

  1. Oligo/anovulation: Irregular or absent ovulation
  2. Hyperandrogenism: Clinical or biochemical signs of excess male hormones
  3. Polycystic ovaries: ≥12 follicles per ovary on ultrasound
Long-term Health Risks
Metabolic Risks
  • Type 2 Diabetes
  • Cardiovascular disease
  • High blood pressure
  • High cholesterol
Reproductive Risks
  • Infertility
  • Pregnancy complications
  • Endometrial cancer risk
